ANIMALS

Introduction

Animals are an important part of our planet. They live in forests, oceans, deserts, and our homes. They help maintain the balance of nature.

Types of Animals

  • 1. Panda

    Pandas are wild animals that mainly eat bamboo.

  • 2. Octobus

    Octobus are sea animals known for their sea adventures.

  • 3. Lion

    Lions are wild animals that live mainly in Africa.

  • 4. Elephant

    Elephants are large mammals with trunks and big ears.

  • 5. Dog

    Dogs are domestic animals and are often kept as pets.
